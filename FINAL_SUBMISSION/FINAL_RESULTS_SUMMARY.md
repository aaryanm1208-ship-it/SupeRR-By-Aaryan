## 3. Key Methodological Innovations

1. **Phase-Adjusted Economy Rate (Solving the Death Overs Bias)**:
   - League average pressure economy in death overs is **10.46 rpo**, whereas middle overs is **8.01 rpo**.
   - Raw economy heavily discriminates against death-overs pacers.
   - We introduced **Runs Saved Above Average per Over (RSAA/over)**: Jasprit Bumrah conceding 7.62 rpo at the death saves **+1.52 runs/over**, perfectly matching Rashid Khan's run-saving rate (+1.52).
2. **Empirical Bayes Shrinkage**:
   - Small sample sizes are statistically shrunk toward population priors ($w_i = n_i / (n_i + K)$) to prevent extreme outlier noise from dominating rankings.
3. **Cluster Bootstrap Resampling (1,000 Draws)**:
   - Resampled at the match level to preserve delivery correlation, generating rigorous 95% confidence bounds.
4. **Zero Future Information Leakage**:
   - All pressure indices use exclusively pre-delivery balls remaining, required run rate, and wickets in hand.
